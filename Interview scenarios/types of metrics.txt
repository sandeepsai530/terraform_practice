- Build Metrics (Build duration, success/failure rate)
- Test Metrics (Test pass/fail rate, coverage %, flaky tests)
- Deployment Metrics (Deployment frequency, rollback count, rollout success rate)
Tools: Jenkins build stats, GitLab CI/CD metrics, ArgoCD/Flux metrics
